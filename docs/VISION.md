# HopIn vision

## The wish

HopIn brings a small circle of people together face to face as easily as opening a link. One person starts a call and shares its link; the others open it in their browser and are in the call, with nothing to install.

A call should feel like sitting at one table: everyone sees and hears everyone, anyone can show their screen or write beside the video, and the table can play together, with an AI quiz master that asks the questions, judges the answers and keeps the score. It should be dependable enough that a short drop does not end the conversation.

What is said and shown belongs to the people in the call. Nobody outside it can watch or listen.

## Who uses it today

The operator and the people he admits. HopIn is made for the friends he invites, who join under terms they accept.

## Deliberate non-goals

- **No anonymous guests.** Only invited people take part; a link alone lets nobody in.
- **No large meetings or broadcasts.** A call is a small circle, not a stage for an audience.
- **No archive or recording.** HopIn keeps no chat history and records nothing; what was said and written is gone when the call ends.
