# Stage

What does HopIn promise a call about the table of a registered app on its stage, and which test proves each rule? The app itself, its chairs and its sign-in are the app's.

The head's decisions, which carry the item's lines O1 A, O2 A and H10:

- ST-01: With an app in `HOPIN_STAGE_APPS`, anyone in a call taps that app's entry in "Mehr" (named by its `title`, for Skat "Skat zeigen"), pastes the link of a table of that app, and every seat of the call, and everyone who joins later, shows that table on the stage. Every joining seat is sent the registered apps by name and title, and a table's frame names its app. Proven by `tests/stage/test_feature.py` (the apps and the state every seat and a late joiner is sent), `frontend/src/lib/features/stage/index.test.ts` (one entry per app, a title taken for text) and `tests/e2e/stage/test_stage_table.py` (a pasted link on every stage and to a later joiner).
- ST-02: Every video moves into a strip at the top and goes on playing; picture-in-picture is off while the table shows, and a held drag is released. Proven by `tests/e2e/stage/test_stage_table.py` (strip, videos, the held tile) and `frontend/src/lib/ui/pip.test.ts`.
- ST-03: The table is removed by the person who brought it or by the host, and, once the person who brought it has left, by anyone. Proven by `tests/stage/test_feature.py` (`TestDetach`, `TestTheOpenerLeaves`) and `tests/e2e/stage/test_stage_table.py`.
- ST-04: Of two attaches at once one wins and the other is told the stage is taken; a detach of an older table removes nothing newer. Proven by `tests/stage/test_feature.py`.
- ST-05: A seat that joins afresh, after a reload or a refused resume, is sent the room's current state and shows that and nothing older; a resume keeps the frame. Proven by `tests/stage/test_feature.py` (`TestFreshJoinsAndTheCallsEnd`) and `frontend/src/lib/features/stage/table.svelte.test.ts` (frames in any order).
- ST-06: The call's end takes the table away. Proven by `tests/stage/test_feature.py` (`TestTheCallsEnd`) and `tests/e2e/stage/test_stage_table.py`.
- ST-07: An empty or unset `HOPIN_STAGE_APPS` means no stage: no item in "Mehr" and `frame-src 'none'`. Set, the page may frame exactly the origin of every entry, and an origin equal to `HOPIN_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` as a browser compares origins refuses the start. Proven by `tests/stage/test_settings.py` and `tests/stage/test_feature_csp.py` (the policy from a two-entry list).
- ST-08: Only a link to a table of a registered app is accepted: its origin equals the entry's, it carries no userinfo, and its path is the entry's `frame_path` with a context key. The frame loads the entry's origin plus `frame_path` with the key in place of `{context}`, plus `?mode=call`, alone; for Skat's entry (`/t/{context}`) that is `{origin}/t/{id}?mode=call`. Proven by `tests/stage/test_settings_links.py`.
- ST-09: The frame runs scripts, but cannot use the camera, the microphone or screen capture, sends no referrer, and cannot navigate the page, open a modal or submit a form. Proven by `tests/e2e/stage/test_stage_table.py` against a stand-in table page.
- ST-10: Removing the stage from the code leaves the call and the other features, and removing the game leaves the stage. Proven by the CI job `Removal drill`, whose legs run the game's and the stage's deletion.
- ST-11: The framed table and the call greet each other, unchanged for every app; `table` stays the name of the context field. The frame sends `{v: 1, type: "ready", table}` once per page document; the call answers every valid one with `{v: 1, type: "hello", attachment, attempt: null}` to the frame's exact origin, so a reloaded document is greeted again. A message counts only from the frame's origin and window, as exactly those three keys in a plain object, with `v` 1, type `ready` and the `table` (the context key) the server named for the attachment now, checked against the origin the attachment's frame address was built from; anything else, a stale `ready` included, is ignored without a word, and the call changes nothing on its own stage. Proven by `frontend/src/lib/features/stage/messages.test.ts` (each ignored form, the accepted one, the `hello` built), `frontend/src/lib/features/stage/table.svelte.test.ts` (the attachment and table id now), `tests/stage/test_feature.py` (`table` on `stage_table`, null without a table) and `tests/e2e/stage/test_stage_table.py` against the stand-in table page (the greeting, a `ready` from another window or for another table ignored, a reloaded document greeted again).
- ST-12: The context key is one bound for every app, `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64}`; HopIn never decodes or logs it, and the app answers an unknown one itself. Proven by `tests/stage/test_settings_links.py` (the bound, not Skat's format).
- ST-13: A malformed `HOPIN_STAGE_APPS` refuses the start in one stderr line naming the setting and never its value. Malformed is: not a JSON list of `{name, title, origin, frame_path}` and nothing else; a `name` outside `[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,31}`; an empty `title`; an `origin` that is not an https origin in a browser's form (lowercase ASCII host, optional port other than 443, no path); a `frame_path` that does not start with `/`, does not hold `{context}` exactly once, or has a `?`, `#`, `.` or `..` segment; a repeated name or origin. Proven by `tests/stage/test_settings_refusal.py`.
- ST-14: `GET /c/new?app=<name>&context=<key>` is the start link of a table, for the apps' own buttons. It sits behind the members gate, and the sign-in sends the member back to the same path and query. It names a registered app once and a key within the ST-12 bound once; anything else, an unknown app included, is a plain 404 that echoes nothing, and without a stage the address is 404 too. When an open call shows that table, it answers a 303 to `/c/<room>`, where the member joins as a guest. Otherwise it answers the page, which opens the host's prejoin, starts a call without a room id and, once the call is created, attaches `(app, context)` to it a single time; a reconnect never attaches again. Proven by `tests/stage/test_start_link.py` (the gate, the three answers, the 404 forms), `tests/web/test_routes.py` (no stage, no page), `frontend/src/lib/ui/navigation.test.ts` (`new` is no room id, the link read from the address), `frontend/src/lib/features/stage/index.test.ts` (one attach, by app and key) and `tests/e2e/stage/test_stage_start_link.py` (the first visit starts a call showing the table, a later one joins it).
- ST-15: Each process keeps one entry per `(app, context)` naming the open call that shows it. The first call to attach a table, by a pasted link or by app and key, takes the entry; a second call that shows the same table by a pasted link does not. A detach or the end of a call removes an entry only when it names that call. A refused attach indexes nothing. The context key is never logged. Proven by `tests/stage/test_feature.py` (`TestAttachByName`, `TestTheOpenCallShowingATable`).
- ST-16: Of the feature routers only the routes with a fixed path are registered ahead of the call core's routes, which is how `/c/new` is answered before `/c/{room_id}`; the routes with a path parameter come after them and never take a core address. Proven by `tests/stage/test_start_link.py` (`TestTheRouteOrder`).
- The joint drive with Skat proves ST-14 to ST-16 through Skat's Anruf key: `scripts/skat_joint_stack.sh <skat checkout> <scratch directory>` starts both apps behind HTTPS and keeps them up for a browser to drive (a first tap starts the call showing the table, a second player's tap joins it, a tap after the call ended starts a new one), and `--smoke` only checks that the hosts answer.

Gaps of the start link, each with its consequence:

- Two first taps within a second make two calls, because neither call shows the table yet when the second visit asks.
- A host who reloads the page between the call's creation and the attach loses the attach: the call exists without the table, and the next visit starts another call.
- A second call that shows the table by a pasted link while another holds the entry is not indexed after the winner detaches, so the start link then starts a new call instead of joining it.
- The index is per process, so with several processes a visit answered by another process starts a call.

An entry's `name` is stable (`docs/configuration.md` says so): it is the app's own contract token, written into the label of its person keys, `hallucinai/<name>/person-key/v1` (byte for byte what Skat writes for `skat`), and into the app's links, so renaming an entry empties every chair of the app. The label is built where seats are placed, which belongs to the slice 2b of overnightworks/hopin#499 with the rest of the chairs.
